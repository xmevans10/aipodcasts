Make a little room for curiosity.

## Review notes draft

No login or account creation is required. Complete or skip onboarding, open an available episode on Home, and tap Play. Episode details contain the transcript and source links. Some shows may not yet have published episodes; select a show with available audio.

To check playback, start an episode, lock the phone, and use Now Playing controls. The app streams audio; a cached catalog does not mean audio has been downloaded. Profile, saved episodes and listening state are stored locally. Settings includes a local-data reset.

Build 12 adds explicit withdrawal notices and replacement links. There are no withdrawn production episodes merely to demonstrate this behavior; the isolated release drill records backend validation separately.

## Privacy page draft

Zwicky does not require an account. The app stores your profile, followed shows, saved episodes, queue, listening progress and listening history on your device. The current app does not integrate an advertising or analytics SDK.

When the app loads its catalog, episode audio or transcripts, it sends network requests to the hosting provider. These requests include network information such as your IP address. Source links open external services with their own privacy practices. The production generation services receive source material and scripts for writing, review or narration; the app does not send your locally stored profile or listening history to those services.

You can remove local app data in Settings. Hosting log retention, the responsible operator, a public contact address and this policy's publication URL must be confirmed before this draft is published.

## Support page draft

For an episode issue, include its title, the show, what happened and the approximate playback time. For playback issues, include your iOS version, whether you were on Wi-Fi or cellular, and whether headphones or Bluetooth were involved. Avoid including API credentials or private personal information.
