## Likely failures, and what they mean

- **`produce` says the app already exists.** Fine. Continue to `beta`.
- **SKU or name collision in `produce`.** The SKU is `zwicky-ios-001`; change it in the
  lane if taken. An app-name collision means "Zwicky" is reserved by another developer;
  stop and ask, do not silently rename the product.
- **No profile / signing failure.** Confirm the API key's team is `8K5ZVPCQ42`. The key is
  team-scoped, not app-scoped, so a key used for another app on the same team is correct.
- **Upload rejected for a duplicate build number.** Bump `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` in
  `ios/project.yml`, commit that bump, and rerun. Do not bump `MARKETING_VERSION`.
- **Asset catalog or size complaints.** The Release app is about 57MB, 38MB of it
  `Assets.car`, from sixteen 1254x1254 1x PNGs. That is within limits. If size must come
  down, add 2x/3x variants or move to HEIC; do not delete covers.
