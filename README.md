# Zwicky

**Big ideas. Easy listening.** A native iPhone science-listening demo and an editorial backend, built from scratch in SwiftUI and Python.

## Try the app

Open the project generated by `xcodegen` in `ios/` (`cd ios && xcodegen`) in Xcode, choose an iPhone simulator, and Run. The build is installed in the iPhone simulator; the simulator was shut down at your request. Start on Today, play a story, choose a host, save an article, and open its sources. No API keys are needed for the clearly labelled demo/device narration.

## What is here

- Native SwiftUI: onboarding, daily edition, topic/search discovery, four hosts, readable stories, source attribution, saved library, started history, mini/full player, sleep and production-audio controls, settings and guarded StoreKit preview.
- OpenAI UI kit token adaptation with MIT attribution; original orbital artwork and editorial visual identity.
- Python: live PLOS Atom/XML ingestion with CC BY checking; OpenAI structured draft adapter; source-quote validation; editorial approval; ElevenLabs TTS adapter; publication/withdrawal; JSON and ranged MP3 serving.
- Product, proposed pricing and economics, marketing copy, launch experiments, architecture, source policy and deployment instructions.

## Voiced episodes (private preview)

Three directed episodes are in [`demos/three-hosts-2026-09-17`](demos/three-hosts-2026-09-17) (`mira.m4a`, `clara.m4a`, `elias.m4a`; open `index.html` to listen). Voices were cast from a 19-voice Eleven v3 audition ([`backend/evals/voices`](backend/evals/voices)). [`backend/produce.py`](backend/produce.py) synthesizes one directed section at a time, caches by content hash so unchanged lines are never re-bought, and mixes locally (intro jingle, transition sting, loudness). [`backend/evals/qa_episodes.py`](backend/evals/qa_episodes.py) transcribes each episode back and diffs it against the script. Human listening and editorial approval are still pending.

## In the app

Zwicky is a platform for four shows, each with its own host: **The Long View** (Mira Vale, space), **Wild Company** (Clara Rowan, nature), **Signal & Noise** (Elias Reed, brain and technology) and **Common Ground** (Theo Mercer, Earth; sample only). Five tabs: **Home** (your shows as cards, continue listening, latest episodes, listening stats), **Browse** (search shows and episodes), **Hosts** (each presenter, their show and how they work), **Library** (followed shows, queue, saved / in progress / played) and **You** (daily goal ring, a week of listening minutes, totals, following and settings). Onboarding is four steps: what this is, pick shows, set a daily goal, play a first episode.

The three voiced episodes ship inside the app (`ios/Zwicky/Episodes`, built by [`backend/bundle_episodes.py`](backend/bundle_episodes.py)). **Read along** opens a transcript set in Charter that highlights each word as it is spoken, follows the narrator and plays from any tapped paragraph; word timings come from ElevenLabs Scribe on the final mix, aligned onto the reviewed script. Host portraits are DiceBear "Notionists" illustrations (CC0). Design notes: [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).
