## Claims

- Replication forks can be stressed by cancer treatments, causing exposed single-stranded DNA and slower synthesis.
  > DNA replication forks can be challenged by cancer chemotherapeutic treatments, leading to accumulation of single-stranded DNA and slowdown of DNA synthesis.
- Cohesin-mediated loop extrusion rearranges contacts at stressed replication forks in a way linked to genome stability.
  > Here we show that cohesin-mediated loop extrusion rearranges nascent DNA contacts at stressed replication forks to promote genome stability.
- The researchers developed a method to capture contacts at newly copied DNA.
  > a newly developed Micro-C-based technique to capture chromatin contacts at nascent DNA (Repli-C)
- Cohesin accumulates at stalled replication forks and shifts contacts away from sister-fork coupling.
  > loop-extruding cohesin accumulates at stalled replication forks, limiting sister-fork coupling in favour of inter-replicon contacts.
- The process promotes fork slowing and reversal by preventing PRIMPOL action on exposed DNA.
  > This process promotes active fork slowing and reversal by preventing PRIMPOL action on single-stranded DNA
